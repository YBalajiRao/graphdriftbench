# Citation policy (supervisor rule)

- No preprints: no arXiv links or entries. Only items with a DOI or a peer-reviewed proceedings record.
- A DOI is required where one exists. Check that each DOI resolves to the claimed title, venue and year before citing; mark anything unchecked `[verify]`.
- `docs/references.bib` is the single source of BibTeX. Do not keep parallel bib files. (Creating or editing it counts as a docs/ change: ask first.)
- In-press 2026/2027 items (e.g. Displays, IPM): cite with the correct journal/year and DOI, flag as in press.
- Known open items from Blueprint §15: TGN (cite via TGB or a stable workshop record, decide with supervisor), Page 1954, Lorden 1971, Batagelj-Zaversnik, Gretton 2012, TGAT/JODIE/DyRep/GraphMixer/DyGFormer/OTGNet conference records.
- Never invent a reference; if a source cannot be verified, say so.
